Privacy Policy

Last updated July 31, 2026

Ardiio pairs you with experienced people at your gym. That only works if a little of your information is shared with them. This page says exactly what we collect, who can see it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

  • We do not sell your data. Not to anyone, ever.
  • We do not show ads and we do not run analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We never see your card number. Stripe handles payments end to end.
  • Location is used only while the app is open, or while you are searching for your gym on this site, and you can skip it either way.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

What we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Email address It is how you sign in. We email you a six digit code instead of using a password.
Your name So the person meeting you at the gym knows who to look for.
Your gym To match you with people who train at the same place.
Bio, specialties, usual hours Optional. Coaches fill these in so trainees know what they can help with.
Approximate location Optional. Used while the app is open to find gyms near you and to tell whether you are at your gym. You can skip this and pick your gym from a list.
Session history and ratings So both sides have a record of what was booked, and so ratings mean something.
Chat messages So you can arrange where to meet. Stored so the thread is still there when you reopen the app.
Invite code To credit the person who invited you and to waive your first booking fee.

That is the whole list. We do not collect your contacts, your photos, your health data, your browsing history, or anything from other apps.

On this website, before the app has launched, the forms ask for less: your email address for the waitlist; your name, email, gym and whether you are joining as a trainee or a coach for early access; and your name, email and message if you use the contact form. We also record that you ticked the box accepting these policies, and which dated version you accepted.

What other people can see

Members at the same gym as you can see your first name, your initials, your gym, and your rating. If you are a coach they can also see your bio, your specialties and the hours you usually train.

Your email address is never shown to another user. Neither is your precise location. Other members only ever see whether you are currently at the gym, not where you are.

Location, specifically

Ardiio asks for location permission once, and only uses it while the app is open on your screen. There is no background location, no geofencing, and no location history. We use it for two things:

  • Finding gyms near you when you first set up your account.
  • Showing a "you are at the gym" indicator so you know the coaches listed are actually there now.

If you say no, the app still works. You pick your gym from a list instead. You can change the permission at any time in your device settings.

On this website, the early access form can list gyms near you as well. If you allow it, your browser gives the page your approximate coordinates, and the page sends those coordinates to the Overpass API — a public service run by the OpenStreetMap community — to ask which gyms are within a few miles. Your coordinates are used for that lookup only. They are not stored by us and are not sent anywhere else, and only the gym you pick is submitted with the form. Decline the prompt and you type your gym in by hand instead; the form works either way.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never sent to, seen by, or stored on Ardiio servers.

Ardiio charges a $1 booking fee per session and keeps 10 percent of the session price. The rest goes to the coach. We keep a record of what each session cost so both sides have a receipt, and Stripe keeps its own records for tax and dispute handling as it is legally required to.

What we do not do

  • No selling or sharing for advertising. Your information is not a product.
  • No third party analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, and no advertising identifier in the app.
  • No AI training. Ardiio has no AI features and your data is not used to train any model. If that ever changes we will update this page and ask you again before it applies to you.
  • No background tracking. Closing the app means the app knows nothing about where you are.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that make Ardiio run. In the app:

  • Stripe for payments and coach payouts.
  • Resend to send your sign in codes by email.
  • Railway where the Ardiio server and database are hosted.

This website is a separate, static site and uses a different set:

  • FormSubmit receives what you type into the early access sign up and the contact form, and forwards it to us by email. That includes your name, email address, gym, and message. It does not receive waitlist sign-ups.
  • Railway receives waitlist sign-ups. Joining the waitlist posts your email address straight to the Ardiio server described above, where it is stored in our own database rather than emailed through a third party.
  • The Overpass API, a public service run by the OpenStreetMap community, receives your approximate coordinates when you use the nearby gym search on the sign up form. It returns a list of gyms and is subject to OpenStreetMap's own privacy practices.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps standard server logs, which include your IP address and browser.

Each of these only receives what it needs to do its job. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example a valid subpoena.

Where your information is held

Ardiio is open to people anywhere, and it is run from the United States. Every provider listed above processes data in the United States, so using Ardiio from outside the US means your information is transferred there, to a country whose privacy law differs from your own and which may not offer the same protections.

If you are in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland and want to know what safeguard applies to a particular transfer, email ethanchangkatytx@gmail.com and we will tell you which one covers it, or tell you plainly if none does yet. We would rather answer that honestly than claim a protection we have not put in place.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your profile, your sessions and your messages from our database straight away. Stripe keeps its own payment records for the period its legal obligations require, which is outside our control.

Deleting your account

You can do this yourself, in the app

Open Ardiio, go to the Profile tab, and tap Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm twice because it cannot be undone. Your profile, your sessions and your messages are permanently removed.

If you cannot get into the app, email ethanchangkatytx@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will do it for you within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, correct it, take a copy of it elsewhere, or have it erased. Email ethanchangkatytx@gmail.com and we will sort it out.

If you are in California, we confirm that we have not sold or shared personal information in the past 12 months, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

If you are in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, you also have the right to object to how we use your information or ask us to restrict it, to receive it in a portable form, and to complain to your national data protection authority. Where we rely on your consent — for launch and marketing emails — you can withdraw it at any time, and doing so does not affect anything we did while it was in place.

Our legal grounds for using your information are: your consent, for launch and marketing emails; performing our agreement with you, to run your account, your bookings and your messages; and our legitimate interests, in replying to people who contact us, keeping the service secure, and understanding which gyms to open in next.

Children

Ardiio is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything meaningful we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how your information is used, ask you to agree again inside the app.

Contact

Questions about any of this go to ethanchangkatytx@gmail.com and a human will answer.
