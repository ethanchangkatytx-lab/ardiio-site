What Ardiio is

Ardiio introduces people at the same gym. Someone experienced (a "coach") can offer to help someone newer (a "trainee") with a spot, a form check, a tour of the machines, or a full session, for a small fee.

Ardiio is an introduction service, not a gym, an employer, or a certifying body. Coaches are independent people, not Ardiio staff. We do not employ them, supervise sessions, or verify professional qualifications.

Who can use it

You must be 16 or older and able to enter a binding agreement. One account per person. Give us accurate information, and keep your email address current, because that is how you sign in.

How money works

  • If someone invited you with a code, your first booking fee is waived.
  • Payments and coach payouts are handled by Stripe. You will also be bound by Stripe's own terms.
