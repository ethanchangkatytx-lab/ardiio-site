Signing in

My code did not arrive

Check your spam folder first, the code comes from codes@ardiio.com. Codes expire after 10 minutes, so if it has been a while, tap "Resend code" for a fresh one. If nothing arrives after two tries, email us and we will check whether the address is being blocked.

It says my code does not match

Codes are six digits and single use. Make sure you are typing the newest one, since requesting a new code cancels the previous one.

I changed my email address

Email us from the old address if you still can, or from the new one with enough detail to identify the account, and we will move it across.

Finding a coach

Nobody is showing at my gym

Ardiio only shows coaches who train at the same gym you picked, and the "here right now" list only shows people who have marked themselves available in the last little while. Early on at a new gym that list can be empty. Try the Schedule tab instead, which posts the time you plan to train so a coach can claim it in advance.

My gym is not in the list

Tap "Find gyms near me" during setup and we will search for gyms around you. If yours still is not there, email us the name and address and we will add it.

Location is not working

Ardiio only uses location while the app is open. If you said no to the permission earlier, turn it back on in your device settings under Ardiio. You never have to use it, you can always pick your gym from the list.

Payments

When am I charged?

Only after a coach accepts your request. A request that gets declined or ignored costs you nothing.

What are the fees?

The session price is set in the app. Ardiio adds a $1 booking fee and keeps 10 percent of the session price. The coach gets the rest. If someone invited you with a code, your first booking fee is free.

My coach did not show up

Email support@ardiio.com within 7 days with the session details and we will refund you. We will not make you argue for it.

I coach and I have not been paid

Payouts run through Stripe and need your Stripe account to be fully set up. Open the Profile or Home tab and check the payouts card. If it says Stripe is still reviewing your details, that is on Stripe's side and usually clears quickly. If it says payouts are active and money still has not arrived after a few days, email us.

Safety

Something felt wrong

Leave the situation first. Then email support@ardiio.com with what happened and who was involved. We read these immediately and we will act on them.

Always meet in the public areas of your gym. Never arrange to meet somewhere private. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

Open Ardiio, go to the Profile tab, and tap Delete my account. You will confirm twice, then your profile, sessions and messages are permanently removed. If you cannot get into the app, email us from the address on the account.

What data do you hold on me?

The Privacy Policy lists it in full. Short version: your email, your name, your gym, whatever you chose to put in your profile, your session history and your messages. No tracking, no ads, no selling.
